FAQ

Fair questions, straight answers.

Will the content actually sound like us?
That is the first thing we build. We study your best-performing content and encode tone, rhythm, vocabulary and formatting into a locked voice profile. You approve the voice on real samples before any pipeline goes live.
Will Google penalize AI-assisted content?
Search engines reward helpful, original content — regardless of how it was drafted. Every piece goes through competitor research, fact checks, plagiarism scans and human approval, with programmatic SEO handled carefully rather than sprayed at scale.
How is this different from just using ChatGPT?
ChatGPT is a blank page. We give you research automation, a locked brand voice, QA guardrails, publishing integrations and a performance feedback loop. The output lands in your CMS and ad platforms — not in a doc someone forgets.
What tools do you integrate with?
WordPress, Webflow, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Meta Ads Manager and the major social schedulers — orchestrated through Make.com and Zapier with Airtable as the content CMS. Custom APIs are straightforward for the Full-Stack build.
How fast can we launch?
The free pilot runs in week one. A single SEO pipeline goes live in about three weeks; the full-stack engine takes four to six weeks including approvals and feedback-loop tuning.
What does the free 1-week test include?
Your brand voice clone, one real pipeline run on your keywords or podcast, and a report comparing output quality, time and cost against your current process. No commitment, no lock-in.
