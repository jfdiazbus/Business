School work

I study Software Engineering at Epitech, from 2025 to 2028. Two projects are worth your time first, then the rest of the pile sits below them.

EpyTodo

A task manager, where you make an account and keep your own list of things to do.

Accounts and sessions are handled properly, and the tasks live in a MySQL database. Built with Node.js, Express and MySQL.

Tardis

A dashboard built from a dataset, with a predictive model behind it.

Cleaning and exploring the data first, then a model that guesses the outcome, then a small dashboard to read it by. Written in Python, in notebooks.
