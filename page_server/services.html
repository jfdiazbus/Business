Questions I get asked

What do you need from me?
Your words, any images you want to use, and a logo if you have one. If you do not have the text ready, I will draft it from a short call and you edit it.
What if I do not like it?
You get one round of changes on the website, and two on the whole service. If the first draft is completely wrong for you, tell me and I refund it instead of asking you to live with it.
Who owns the code?
You do, once it is paid for. It is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so any developer can pick it up later without asking me for anything.
Do I need an account anywhere?
For a website, no. For an API, whatever it talks to decides that, and I will tell you what is needed before starting.
Can I pay differently?
Payments are single payments through Stripe, by card or whichever other method it offers you, and every payment comes with an invoice. If you need an invoice before you pay, email me and we will sort it out.
What happens after I pay?
Stripe emails your receipt, I get the order with anything you wrote in it, and I reply within one working day with the first step and any questions I still have.
