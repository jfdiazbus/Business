Terms and privacy

Who you are dealing with

Juan Felipe Diaz Bustamante, working on my own from France. You can reach me at juan-felipe.diaz-bustamante@epitech.eu, and I answer within one working day.

What these terms cover

The three fixed price services on the prices page: a website, the small service behind it, and the whole website service. What each price includes is written there, in the same words I work to. If something here is unclear, email me before you pay and I will explain it.

Prices and paying

Prices are in euro and fixed before you pay. Tax is added at checkout where it applies. Payment goes through Stripe, by card or whichever other method it offers you, and every payment comes with an invoice. Stripe handles your card details; I never see them.

When you get it

A website takes five working days from the day I have your content. A small service takes about a week from the day we agree what it has to do. The whole service takes two to three weeks, depending on how quickly we agree on the design. If a date slips because of something on my side, I tell you as soon as I know.

Changes and refunds

You get one round of changes on a website, and two on the whole service. If you want more than that, ask me and I will tell you what it costs before I do it. If the first draft is completely wrong for you, tell me and I refund it instead of asking you to live with it.

Cancelling, if you are a consumer in the European Union

If you are a consumer, you can usually cancel within 14 days of the day you paid, without giving a reason. Email me and say so.

If you ask me to start before those 14 days are over, and I have already done part of the work, you pay for the part that is done and I refund the rest. If the work is finished and you have what you paid for, there is nothing left to cancel.

If you are a business rather than a consumer, that 14 day right does not apply to you. Give me your VAT number and the invoice shows a reverse charge where that applies.

Who owns what

Once you have paid, the code and the words I wrote for you are yours. Any developer can pick them up afterwards without asking me for anything. I keep the right to mention the work and show it in my portfolio, unless you ask me not to.

What I am responsible for

I do the work carefully and to the standard described on the prices page. If something goes wrong, my responsibility is limited to what you paid me for that piece of work. Nothing here takes away a right you have under the law where you live, and nothing here covers a delay or a failure caused by something outside my control.

Your data

I hold your name, your email address, what you wrote in the brief, and the records of what you paid. Stripe holds the payment itself and sends you the receipt. I use your details to do the work, to send the invoice, and to keep the accounting records I have to keep.

I do not sell your data, and I do not send you marketing. Email me to ask for a copy of what I hold, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted where the law allows. That last part matters: I have to keep invoices for a while, so anything attached to them stays until that period is over. The private blog editor also keeps a one way hash of a network address when someone tries to sign in, to slow password guessing; expired entries are cleared on a later sign in attempt.

Which law applies

The law of the country I work from applies, which is France. If you live elsewhere in the European Union, you keep the consumer rights your own country gives you, and nothing on this page narrows them.

Changes to these terms

The version published on the day you pay is the one that applies to your work. If I change anything, this page shows the date it last changed. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Not sure about any of this?

Ask me before you pay rather than after. That is what the email address is for.
