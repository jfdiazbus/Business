The gap

Every company wants AI. Almost none have it in production.

The models are ready. The bottleneck is architecture, integration and ownership. These are the four reasons AI projects stall.

🧠

Generic chats don’t know your business

No SOPs, no CRM context, no product data. A chatbot that can’t see your systems gives answers nobody can act on.

🎯

Hallucination & context limits

Long documents blow the context window and one confident wrong answer erodes team trust for months.

🔌

The integration gap

AI sits in a browser tab while the actual work lives in HubSpot, Slack, Drive and the ERP. No integration, no leverage.

🔁

Model churn is nobody’s job

GPT-4o today, Claude tomorrow, a cheaper model next quarter. Without ownership, prompts silently break.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you need access to our private data?
We start in a sandbox with samples, then move to least-privilege access with PII redaction at the edge. Everything can run inside your cloud or VPC, and we sign DPAs before touching production data.
Which models do you use?
Whatever wins on your evals: GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini Pro — or open-weight models for cost-sensitive, high-volume tasks. A cost-aware router sends each request to the cheapest model that passes your quality bar.
How fast can we see something live?
The audit surfaces quick wins in 30 minutes. A sprint ships a production workflow in two weeks, and prototypes are often usable within days.
What if the AI gets something wrong?
High-risk actions go through human-in-the-loop approvals. Everything else is covered by evals, grounded retrieval with citations, confidence thresholds and full audit logs.
Do we own the prompts and workflows?
Yes. All prompts, workflows and documentation are handed over and yours to keep. No lock-in — you can bring everything in-house at any time.
How does the $500/month retainer work?
It covers prompt optimization, A/B tests, model migrations, cost tuning and monitoring, plus a monthly optimization report. Cancel anytime; most clients keep it because the stack keeps improving.
